Exceptions

Exceptions raised by improv-client.

Every exception derives from ImprovError, so a single except ImprovError catches anything the library raises on purpose.

exception improv_client.exceptions.ImprovNotSupportedError[source]

Bases: ImprovError

The device does not support the requested command.

Raised before sending when the BLE capabilities say the command isn’t supported, and when the device answers with UNKNOWN_RPC_COMMAND.
