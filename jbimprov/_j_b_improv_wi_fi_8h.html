Detailed Description

The Wi-Fi interface JBImprov uses to connect and scan.

ESP32 and ESP8266 get a ready made implementation, JBImprovDefaultWiFi. On other boards, derive a class from JBImprovWiFi and pass it to the JBImprov constructor.
